Five type sets from Awwwards’ 100 Best Free Fonts 2025, each on the site’s real components (coal.css), with copy from the homepage and the radio-telescope article. The site keeps its three roles: a display face for titles and names, a prose face, and a mono UI voice for labels, figures and links. Set 0 is what the site uses today. In sets 1–5 the opening title moves from mono to the display face; the masthead and every label stay in the set’s mono.
