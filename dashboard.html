05 / Colophon

Where all of this comes from.

Nothing on this page is scraped and nothing is stored. Each widget reads a public API on load; the two that browsers will not call directly go through a small Flask service of mine on Cloud Run.

  • Rail

    Positions and routes from Irish Rail's realtime API, proxied for CORS and polled once a minute. Basemap tiles from CARTO and Esri.

  • Solar

    X-ray flux from NOAA SWPC's GOES feed. Images from NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory, roughly fifteen minutes behind the Sun.

  • Ephemeris

    Sunrise, sunset and day length from sunrise-sunset.org and Visual Crossing; lunar phase from icalendar37.

  • Papers

    The newest flare-star preprints from the arXiv API, cached by the same Flask service.
