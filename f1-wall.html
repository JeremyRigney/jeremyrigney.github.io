Settings

Keep the screen on

While a session is live, so a wall beside the TV doesn’t go to sleep mid-race.

Voice alerts

A tone, then the flag said out loud as the wall shows it. Set the delay a second ahead of your stream to hear it just before the picture.

Reminders

A notification an hour before each session you pick, while this page is open in a tab. To be reminded with it closed, add the weekend to your calendar from the poster between sessions.

Signed in

This browser stays signed in until you sign out.

Leave this tab in sight during a session. Out of sight for a minute, it gives up the live stream and checks for news every 30 seconds.
