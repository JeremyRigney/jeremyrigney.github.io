01 / What I do

From terabyte-scale telescope data to production models.

Background

I’m a Senior Data Scientist at Optum, with a PhD in astrophysics from Queen’s University Belfast. My research centred on observing the Sun and other stars to better understand stellar dynamics, which meant designing and running analysis pipelines end to end on terabytes of telescope data.

I’ve led collaborative projects spanning multiple continents, worked in Agile software teams, and published peer-reviewed research. I’m also an experienced science communicator who has represented Ireland and won awards for public engagement.
