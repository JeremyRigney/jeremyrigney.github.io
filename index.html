    01 / What I do

    Agentic AI in healthcare.

    Background

    I’m a Senior Data Scientist at Optum. I design and build agentic AI systems for a regulated healthcare setting. That includes MCP data contracts, which set out what data an agent can access and in what form, and grounding, so that answers are based on trusted data. A large part of the work is evaluation and testing, to check these systems behave as expected before they are used.

    I also work closely with stakeholders to agree what a good result looks like and to help shape where projects go. Most of this work is internal, so I can’t share much detail here. I’m happy to talk about the general approach.

    Before Optum I was a Data Scientist at LexisNexis Risk Solutions. Before that I did a PhD in astrophysics at Queen’s University Belfast, where I built pipelines for terabytes of telescope data and led collaborative projects across several continents.

    I’m also an award-winning science communicator and have represented Ireland in public engagement.
